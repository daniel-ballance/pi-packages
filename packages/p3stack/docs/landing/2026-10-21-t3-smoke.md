## Findings

- T3 worktrees created before #64 keep a project setting that loads `../packages/self-reload`. Pi then finds two `self_reload` tools and exits 1, and T3 reports `Pi RPC read failed: pi process exited with code 1`. Fast-forwarding a clean, merged branch to `main` clears the setting. On Omarchy, 24 such worktrees were fast-forwarded. The 15 with unmerged commits or a detached head still have the setting.
- The Mac's `~/.pi/agent/extensions/` held July and August copies of computer-use, subagents, todo, mcp-client and auto-caffeinate. They were removed, leaving `herdr-agent-state.ts`, which matches Omarchy.
