#### Roles and placement

Every role below is delegated per the [host capabilities](references/host-capabilities.md). Its **Nesting** section decides whether sub-coordinators exist on your host.

- **Coordinator (this chat).** Frames, authors briefs, drains the inbox, owns the human report, makes judgment calls. It never authors or edits code. Conflicted merges, chain rebases, and code changes are always tasks. Mechanically landing a verified unit (fast-forward or clean cherry-pick of a worker's commit, then push) is bookkeeping the coordinator may do itself on repos where local git is cheap. Queueing finished work behind an idle stacker is how a deadline harvests nothing. The loop is agentic end to end. Agents are spawned, resumed, and drained only through delegation (**Delegate**). Pick each subagent's reasoning level for the job, per the Subagents section. State reads and writes go through `scripts/orch/orch.ts` at drain points, one command in and one line out. The CLI never spawns, waits, or wakes anything.
- **Sub-coordinator.** Durable, one per track, and only when the program exceeds what one coordinator's drains can manage and your host allows a subagent to delegate (**Nesting**). Where it does not, the coordinator drains every track itself, or runs a track as a workflow. A track the coordinator can drain itself needs no middle layer. Each nested layer re-pays a full orientation preamble, and a blocking sub-coordinator hides its children while the parent idles. Owns its track's units and boards, authors its workers' briefs, spawns its own workers and verifiers. Rolls up aggregates at wave boundaries. Never forwards raw child reports. Cap in-flight children at what one drain can process, roughly ten, as a rolling window, and never above the live-child limit. Never as blocking batches, which cost the slowest child of every batch.
- **Worker / verifier.** Each writer gets its own checkout (**Isolation**). All work runs on this machine, so a worker can drive the real app (**Drive the app**), read local Pi session files, and use local auth. Briefs still inline what a worker needs or point at repo paths, because a worker starts without your context. Prefer fewer, broader workers. One writer per worktree or branch (principle-separate-before-serializing-shared-state). Run a unit's verifier on a different model family from its worker.
